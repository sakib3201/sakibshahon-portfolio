# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads, and founders evaluating candidates for AI-first engineering roles. They scan fast, looking for signal over noise. They've seen dozens of portfolios and are filtering for genuine technical depth, not polish for its own sake.

## Product Purpose

Sakib Ahamed Shahon's software engineering portfolio. Exists to demonstrate agentic engineering and AI architecture capability through shipped work, not claims. Success is a hiring decision or project conversation triggered by what they see here.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, confident. The terminal/protocol aesthetic is not decoration. It is a statement: this person thinks in systems, communicates in specifications, and ships with intention.

## Anti-references

Generic developer portfolios: two-column hero with headshot, gradient backgrounds, "passionate developer" copy, progress bars for skill percentages, testimonial sliders, loading screens, scroll-jacking, or any template that could belong to anyone.

## Design Principles

1. **Signal through structure.** The protocol framing (section labels like `// EXPERIENCE`, form fields like `IDENTIFIER [NAME]`) is itself proof of systems thinking. Every structural choice carries meaning.
2. **Precision is personality.** Every word, label, and spacing decision is deliberate. Nothing is filler. The restraint is the confidence.
3. **Work over wrapper.** Projects, experience, and skill are the spectacle. Design serves clarity of that content, never competes with it.
4. **Expert confidence.** No hedging, no modest disclaimers, no softening. The portfolio speaks with the authority of someone who has done the work.
5. **Earn every pixel.** If a section, element, or animation does not actively help the visitor decide to hire, it does not ship.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. The dark theme must maintain sufficient contrast ratios for all text, especially monospace labels at small sizes. Consider reduced motion preferences for the typing and scroll-triggered animations.
